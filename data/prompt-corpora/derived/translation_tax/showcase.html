The translation tax, returned

For a generation we compressed our intentions into keywords a search engine could parse. Set the same people in front of a chatbot and they stop compressing: they write in sentences, they say I and you, they put the grammar back. Here is that difference, drawn from 356,930 real messages.

Why you can trust these numbers

Every figure survived the recut

A single copied prompt template once faked a striking cultural finding. So no number ships until it is recomputed with circulating templates stripped out. This one barely moves — it is a fact about the language, not an artefact of what got pasted.

MeasureRawFreehandΔ

The politeness finding moved 74% → 6% on the same recut. This one moved one word.

That collapse is why the gate exists. A structural claim about how people write survives decontamination; a prevalence-by-country claim did not. The gate is now a standing rule for the whole workstream.
