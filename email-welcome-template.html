Welcome.

The Unconscious Consumer is a newsletter about the gap between what people say they'll do and what they actually do. Consumer psychology, behavioural economics, and fifteen years of UX research all live in that gap. It turns out to be surprisingly wide.

Each piece takes one idea and works it through properly. The mechanism, the evidence, what it means in practice. No summaries of things you could Google.

New pieces go out roughly every two weeks. Here's a good place to start:
 

Consumer Psychology

The Endowment Effect: Why We Overvalue What We Own

Once we own something, we value it more than we did before we owned it. This piece follows the mechanism and what it means for pricing, product returns, and why people keep things they should send back.
