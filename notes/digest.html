Software factories and code reviews with Harjot Gill

Your angle
Shipping 5,000 PRs a month assumes the bottleneck was ever human attention rather than human trust. People do not adopt tools when they are better, they adopt them when they stop being asked to verify what they cannot feel responsible for, and that friction is behavioural, not technical.
