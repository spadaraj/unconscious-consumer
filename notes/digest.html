Your angle
Parents who claim to value equal opportunity but relocate for the 'right' district are the purest example of the say-do gap: the 'good school' is a positional good, and what they are buying is peer sorting, not pedagogy. Worth asking whether the product being purchased is education at all or just proximity to the right neighbours.
