Incentives are for losers

Published 2026-08-05
OR: you must look at this fish and go insane
Directly challenges incentive theory, core to behavioural economics and choice architecture, with strong room for a contrarian take.
Your angle
The dirty secret of incentive design is that it assumes people are stable preference machines waiting to be nudged, when most of the time they act first and rationalise the incentive later. If your product only works when the reward is switched on, you have not changed behaviour, you have rented it.
