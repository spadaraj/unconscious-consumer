Note 2 hook
Cognitive biases aren't flaws in human reasoning. They're features. Present bias, loss aversion, the lot of them made perfect sense for ancestors who couldn't count on living to collect a distant reward. We're running ancient software on a modern operating system.
From: In Defence of Behavioural Economics
