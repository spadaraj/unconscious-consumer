Note 1 hook
The ad that made you tear up did not sell you a product. It rented your empathy. When you watch someone enjoy something, your brain simulates the enjoyment as if it were yours. Marketers learned this before neuroscientists named it.

Note 2 stat
A returning visitor already told you something: they came back. One automotive client showed them the exact car configuration from their last visit. Nothing pushy, just "we remember where you were." Result: 15% more leads. Most sites throw that signal away.

Note 3 pattern
Presidential candidates with lower voices won the popular vote in every US election from 1960 to 2002. We think we vote on policy. We're partly voting on evolutionary hardware that reads a deep voice as dominance and trust.
