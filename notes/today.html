Note 2 pattern
The pattern behind influencer marketing isn't reach, it's framing. The same claim lands as advertising from a brand and as advice from a person. We don't scrutinise friends the way we scrutinise sellers, so the format does the persuading before the product ever gets a look.
From: The Influencer Tipping Point: How Social Media Personalities Are Steering Consumer Behaviour
It names a cross-case pattern (framing over reach) and ties it to a behavioural mechanism, giving the Note a clear POV that stands alone.
