Note 2 example

Note 3 pattern
The pattern repeats everywhere: streaming catalogues, restaurant menus, retirement plans. Add options past a certain point and satisfaction drops, not because people can't choose, but because they stop trusting the choice they made.
From: Navigating the Paradox of Choice in Today's Streaming Wars
It lifts the streaming example into a cross-industry pattern and lands on the real cost of overchoice, which is post-decision regret rather than paralysis alone.
